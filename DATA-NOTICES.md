The software's MIT license is in LICENSE.md. It does not grant rights to
third-party corpora, texts, fonts or dependencies. This notice is an inventory
of unresolved redistribution checks, not a substitute license or legal clearance.

Datasets are downloaded separately on request. Before redistribution, verify
the exact source, edition, attribution and license for each selected corpus.

- Voynich transcription: https://github.com/chirila/Voynich-public . Source
  credits Lindemann and Bowern; the workbench selects the Takahashi H line.
  An explicit redistribution grant for the exact corpus and bundled test
  fixture has not yet been established in this handover review.
- CLTK Latin Library mirror: its source license references Public Domain Mark
  1.0 with jurisdiction and other-rights caveats. Preserve exact provenance.
- https://github.com/christos-c/bible-corpus : repository CC0 declaration does
  not itself clear third-party translation rights. Check the selected editions.
- https://github.com/morphgnt/sblgnt : SBLGNT text terms are separate from the
  CC-BY-SA 3.0 parsing/lemmatization data. Do not label the corpus MIT.
- Project Gutenberg texts: https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html .
  Check each ebook's included notice, jurisdiction and trademark conditions.

Python and Electron dependency notices must be checked in each final artifact.
Bundling this project notice does not establish that every dependency notice
is present. Public release remains gated on the complete notice inventory and
source-specific corpus rights review.
